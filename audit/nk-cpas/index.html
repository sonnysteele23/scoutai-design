The five findings that matter

01

Mobile hero takes 32.2 s to paint (Lighthouse mobile LCP 32.2 s; desktop LCP 6.0 s).

Google's 'good' threshold is 2.5 s. On a phone, a prospect sees a blank hero for half a minute - most leave. Performance score survives at 75 only because everything else is fast.

02

Google has no machine-readable business card.

The only schema on the home page is "@type":"WebSite". No LocalBusiness/AccountingService block with the 999 Bishop St address, (808) 524-2255 or hours - so AI answers and the Maps knowledge panel have nothing to cite from the site itself.

03

Zero analytics.

No GA4 (G-...), GTM or Meta pixel ID appears in the page source. The firm cannot see which pages produce the 'Connect' form submissions or calls.

04

Phone is not reachable above the fold on mobile: the single `tel:` link sits in the footer (not within the first 20% of the HTML).

Prospects on a phone must scroll past the whole page to tap-call.

05

Stale trust signals: footer copyright reads '© 2024' (two years old); logo images carry empty alt text; no hours anywhere in visible text.

These are measurements, not design opinions. Each one is something your current provider could have run in ten minutes.

What I would do in the first week

  • Add a LocalBusiness/AccountingService JSON-LD block (name, 999 Bishop St Suite 2200, (808) 524-2255, hours, sameAs for the existing Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn/Maps links) - Duda's SEO panel supports custom head code.
  • Install GA4 + GTM; mark the Connect form submit and tel: taps as conversions.
  • Add a sticky 'Call (808) 524-2255' button in the Duda header for mobile.
  • Fix the hero: compress/resize the LCP image or swap the hero video for a poster image; set fetchpriority=high.
  • Alt text on the two logo images; update the footer year to 2026; add office hours in the footer.
