The five findings that matter

01

The main call-to-action is broken: the 'Management Services' button on the home page (`href="/owner/management-services"`) returns HTTP 404.

An owner who wants to hire Cadmus clicks it and gets an error page.

02

The title tag is anonymous: `<title>Professional Property Management</title>` - 32 characters, no 'Cadmus', no 'Honolulu'.

Google shows that line in results; it does not say who you are or where. The description (92 chars) is also under-length.

03

Phones are not tappable: (808) 531-6847 and (808) 900-7415 appear as plain text (`tel_links: []`).

On mobile the number must be copied by hand.

04

No structured data and no About page: zero JSON-LD blocks; no team or history page was discoverable from the home page.

For a 40-year-old firm that is an invisible track record - AI assistants and Google have nothing to cite.

05

No analytics of any kind and no social or review links.

The firm cannot measure owner enquiries, and there is no outside proof (Google reviews, LinkedIn) linked from the site.

These are measurements, not design opinions. Each one is something your current provider could have run in ten minutes.
