The five findings that matter

01

The website's own title tag publishes the wrong phone number: `<title>Kailua Dermatology Associates, Ltd | Kailua, HI | 808-291-6133</title>` while the real number is 808-261-6133 (which appears 13 times in the page; 291 appears 3 times).

That title is what Google shows in search results - patients are dialling a wrong number straight from Google.

02

Typing the domain without 'www' fails.

https://kailuadermatologyassociates.com/ and http://kailuadermatologyassociates.com/ do not connect (curl exit 7); only www. works. Every business card, referral or voice-assistant that says 'kailuadermatologyassociates dot com' dead-ends.

03

Google is pointed at a staging server.

robots.txt says Sitemap: http://bsbv8886-s63v97rmbm4mbbul.website.thryv.com/sitemap.xml and the sitemap's four <loc> entries are all on that thryv.com host, last modified 2022-08-31. The real domain has no valid sitemap.

04

No business schema: the only JSON-LD is `WebSite`.

Address, phone and hours exist as text but nothing machine-readable, and there is no Analytics at all - the practice cannot tell whether the site produces any calls.

05

Thin and under-structured: 4 pages, 0 H2 headings, 12 of 21 images with empty alt (several are 'invisible background' spacer PNGs), no copyright year, no reviews or testimonials anywhere.

These are measurements, not design opinions. Each one is something your current provider could have run in ten minutes.

  • Add MedicalClinic/Physician JSON-LD with NAP + hours; install GA4.
