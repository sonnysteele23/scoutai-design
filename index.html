Questions people ask

Straight answers.

I already have a site on Wix or GoDaddy. Why change it?

If it loads fast on a phone, shows your phone number above the fold, and you are getting calls from Google, keep it. If any of those is a no, that is the fix. I will tell you honestly on the free look which one you are.

Do I have to pay monthly?

Not for the website. It is a one-time build and you own it. The Google Business Profile service is monthly because it is ongoing work, and you can cancel any month.

What is the "three businesses at a discount" thing?

I am new on Oahu and want local work to show. The first three businesses this month get a reduced build price in exchange for letting me use the finished site in my portfolio. Nothing else changes.

Can you write the words? I am not a writer.

Yes. We talk for about 20 minutes about what you do and who calls you, and I write everything from that. You approve it before it goes live.

Will it show up on Google?

A new site does not rank overnight, and anyone who promises page one in a week is guessing. What I do is build it right from day one, titles, local pages, speed, and your Google Business Profile, which is where most local calls come from.
